About Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka, officially the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, is an island country in South Asia. It lies in the Indian Ocean, southwest of the Bay of Bengal and southeast of the Arabian Sea; it is separated from the Indian subcontinent by the Gulf of Mannar and the Palk Strait. Sri Lanka is known for its stunning beaches, ancient ruins, diverse wildlife, and lush tea plantations.

Popular Destinations

Sigiriya

An ancient rock fortress and palace, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Kandy

Home to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic and a cultural hub.

Galle

A historic fortified city with a charming colonial atmosphere.

Culture & Heritage

Sri Lanka's culture is rich and diverse, influenced by Buddhism, Hinduism, and colonial heritage. Traditional arts, dance, music, and festivals play a significant role in daily life. The cuisine is also a highlight, known for its aromatic spices and fresh ingredients.
